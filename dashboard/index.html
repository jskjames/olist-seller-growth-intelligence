READ THE FINE PRINT / 05

Measurement rules.

01 · LEAD GRAIN

One row per marketing-qualified lead. A 90-day win counts only when the deal’s date is within 90 days of first contact. No raw top-of-funnel traffic is available.

02 · SELLER GRAIN

One row per seller who won within 90 days of first contact, with a complete 90-day selling window by 31 August 2018. No recorded eligible order means zero observed sales, not proof the seller never sold.

03 · MONEY GRAIN

Delivered order items, joined to orders on order ID and to won deals by seller ID. GMV sums item price only and excludes freight. It is not net revenue, profit or an attribution estimate.

04 · UNCERTAINTY

Wilson intervals describe lead conversion. Bootstrap intervals describe a seller cohort’s mean observed GMV. Small cohorts are not suitable for strong channel-ranking claims.
